AI Agents & Integrations

Autonomous agents that act inside EHR and EMR: triage, prior authorization, scheduling, documentation, and cross-system data orchestration.

Model Fine-Tuning

Foundation models adapted to your proprietary clinical data — speaking your specialty, following your protocols, performing on your patient population.

Compliance & AI Governance
